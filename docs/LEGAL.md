# EMY CAM — Terms of Use & Responsible-Use Policy (draft)

> This is a product draft to be reviewed and finalized by qualified legal
> counsel before release. It is not legal advice.

## Acknowledgment shown on first run

By using EMY CAM you agree that:

1. **You will not impersonate any real person without their explicit permission.**
   Using another person's face or voice to deceive, defraud, harass, or
   misrepresent is prohibited and may be illegal in your jurisdiction.
2. **All output is AI-generated.** EMY CAM labels processed video and audio as
   AI-generated. You will not remove, obscure, or misrepresent this labeling.
3. **You are responsible for how you use the output**, including compliance with
   the terms of any platform (e.g. video-call or streaming services) you use it
   with.

## Reporting misuse

If someone uses EMY CAM to impersonate you or another person without consent,
it can be reported to us. Confirmed misuse can result in the offending account or
installation being **blocked and restricted**, and may be referred to the
appropriate authorities.

- Report channel: _to be configured_
- What to include: description, evidence, and (if known) the identity of the
  offender.

## Labeling

Every processed stream includes an on-screen "AI-generated" indicator, and
recorded files include an AI-generated metadata tag. This labeling is a core,
non-optional feature.

## Data & privacy

- Media is processed locally on the user's device.
- A local audit log records which avatar/model was active and when, to support
  abuse investigation. See the Settings tab for retention controls.
